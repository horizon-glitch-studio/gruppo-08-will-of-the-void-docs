# Glossary

The official terms of Will of the Void. Every other file, the website, the interface and the code use these names and no others. The Italian column is for the team only.

Rarities and factions are always singular, also when they describe a group: "a Captive card", "an Elect creature", "the Lava limit".

## World

| Term | Italian | Definition | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Umbra | Umbra | The galaxy where the game takes place. | – |
| Void | Vuoto | The conscious black hole at the center of Umbra. It feeds on the Energy of battles. It is also the voice of the interface: victory, defeat and error messages speak with its tone. | – |
| Arena | Arena | The place inside the Void where creatures fight. Every match happens in the Arena. | [match.md](match.md) |
| Summoner | Evocatore | The player. A Summoner commands a Ship and sends its creatures to fight in the Arena. | – |
| Ship | Navicella | The Summoner's base, in orbit around the Void. The creatures found in packs live on board. | – |
| Event horizon | Orizzonte degli eventi | The narrative place of a Summoner with 0 Trophies: on the edge of the Void, not swallowed by it. Nobody is ever swallowed. | [ranking.md](ranking.md) |

## Cards

| Term | Italian | Definition | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Card | Carta | A creature. Every card in the game is a creature, with ATK, DEF, a faction and a rarity. "Card" and "creature" are the same thing. | [cards-and-rarities.md](cards-and-rarities.md) |
| ATK | Attacco | The damage a creature deals to the opposing creature in each round. Always at least 1. | [cards-and-rarities.md](cards-and-rarities.md) |
| DEF | Difesa | The damage a creature can take. It goes down with damage and never regenerates during a match. | [cards-and-rarities.md](cards-and-rarities.md) |
| Points | Punti | ATK + DEF of a card. Fixed by the rarity. Internal rule for card design, not shown to the player. | [cards-and-rarities.md](cards-and-rarities.md) |
| Faction | Fazione | The element a creature is made of. It decides the advantages against other factions. | [factions.md](factions.md) |
| Rarity | Rarità | The tier of a card. It decides its points and how many copies a deck can hold. The only measure of strength. | [cards-and-rarities.md](cards-and-rarities.md) |
| Collection | Collezione | All the cards a Summoner owns, with their copies. | [cards-and-rarities.md](cards-and-rarities.md) |
| Memory archive | Archivio dei ricordi | The part of the collection where departed Wanderer cards stay. Visible, not playable. | [cards-and-rarities.md](cards-and-rarities.md) |

## Factions

| Term | Italian | Definition | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lava | Lava | Elemental faction. Creatures of magma from planets close to the stars. | [factions.md](factions.md) |
| Electro | Electro | Elemental faction. Creatures of conductive materials and electromagnetic fields. | [factions.md](factions.md) |
| Radio | Radio | Elemental faction. Creatures of radioactive, unstable elements. | [factions.md](factions.md) |
| Ice | Ice | Elemental faction. Creatures of ice and frozen liquids from planets far from the stars. | [factions.md](factions.md) |
| Metal | Metal | Elemental faction. Creatures of metal, often magnetic. | [factions.md](factions.md) |
| Matte | Matte | Elemental faction. Creatures of dark carbon and opaque minerals. | [factions.md](factions.md) |
| Gem | Gemma | Inert faction. Crystals formed in the core of planets. Every Gem card is an Elect card, and every Elect card is a Gem card. | [factions.md](factions.md) |
| Noble Gas | Gas nobile | Inert faction. Creatures born in nebulae and between galaxies. Every Noble Gas card is a Wanderer card, and every Wanderer card is a Noble Gas card. | [factions.md](factions.md) |
| Elemental faction | Fazione elementale | Lava, Electro, Radio, Ice, Metal or Matte. Elemental factions give and receive advantages. | [factions.md](factions.md) |
| Inert faction | Fazione inerte | Gem or Noble Gas. An inert faction never gives or receives advantages. | [factions.md](factions.md) |
| Advantage | Vantaggio | The modifier a faction gets against a faction it prevails over. It lasts for the whole clash. | [factions.md](factions.md) |
| Even | Alla pari | Two factions with no advantage between them. Two creatures of the same faction are always even. | [factions.md](factions.md) |

| Term | Italian | Definition | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Deck | Mazzo | A set of cards from the collection, saved by the Summoner and used in a match. It must respect the deck constraints. | [decks.md](decks.md) |
| Valid deck | Mazzo valido | A deck that respects every deck constraint and uses only cards the Summoner owns and can play. | [decks.md](decks.md) |
| Starter deck | Mazzo iniziale | The deck every Summoner receives at registration. The same for everyone, with cards of every elemental faction. | [decks.md](decks.md) |
| Defense deck | Mazzo di difesa | The deck the system uses when other Summoners attack in Ranked. Required to play Ranked. | [decks.md](decks.md) |
| Shop | Negozio | The section of the portal where Summoners buy packs with Shards. | [packs.md](packs.md) |
| Pack | Pacchetto | A set of random cards bought with Shards. Opening a pack sends a call: new creatures answer and board the Ship. | [packs.md](packs.md) |
| Standard pack | Pacchetto standard | The pack always on sale. It never contains Wanderer cards. | [packs.md](packs.md) |
| Event pack | Pacchetto evento | A pack on sale for a limited period. It can contain Wanderer cards. | [packs.md](packs.md) |
| Slot | Slot | A position in a pack, with its own rarity probabilities. | [packs.md](packs.md) |
| Guarantee | Garanzia | A rule that grants a card of a given rarity after a number of packs without one. | [packs.md](packs.md) |

## Match

| Term | Italian | Definition | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Match | Partita | A battle between two decks, simulated by the server from start to end without choices from the Summoners. | [match.md](match.md) |
| Clash | Scontro | The fight between the two creatures in the Arena. It lasts one or more rounds and ends when at least one creature dies. | [match.md](match.md) |
| Round | Round | One exchange inside a clash: both creatures hit each other at the same time. | [match.md](match.md) |
| Effective ATK | Attacco effettivo | The ATK a creature uses in a clash, after the faction advantage. | [match.md](match.md) |
| Energy | Energia | The match score. A Summoner gains Energy when its creature defeats the opposing one. The first to reach the win threshold wins. It replaces the life points of the brief. | [match.md](match.md) |
| Win threshold | Soglia di vittoria | The Energy needed to win a match. It depends on the mode. | [match.md](match.md) |
| Deck exhaustion | Mazzo esaurito | The moment a deck has no cards left to deploy. The match ends. | [match.md](match.md) |
| End bonus | Bonus di fine mazzo | The Energy given for every card a Summoner still has, the card in the Arena included, when the match ends by deck exhaustion. | [match.md](match.md) |
| Draw | Pareggio | A match that ends with the same Energy for both Summoners. | [match.md](match.md) |
| Replay | Replay | The turn-by-turn record of a match, visible to both Summoners. | [match.md](match.md) |

| Term | Italian | Definition | Rules |

## Rationale

- **One name per concept.** Players, the team and the code use the same words, so the website, the interface, the API and the tests never drift apart.
- **Singular rarities and factions.** "An Elect card" reads naturally in English and avoids irregular plurals in the interface.
- **Energy instead of life points.** Energy fills up instead of draining, and fits the lore: every battle feeds the Void. The scoring is the same as the life points of the brief, read the other way round; the change was approved by the teachers. See [match.md](match.md).
