# Cookie Policy
**Which cookies this site uses and how to choose.**  
**Last updated:** November 16, 2026

## What cookies are
Cookies and similar browser storage technologies (such as `localStorage` and `sessionStorage`) are small text files stored on your device when you visit **Will of the Void**. They help the portal function properly, keep your Summoner session secure, remember your cookie preferences, and help us understand how players interact with the site.

## Necessary cookies
Necessary cookies are strictly required for the core operation and security of the portal. Because the game cannot function without them, they are always active and do not require your consent:
* **Authentication & Session Management:** Keep you logged into your Summoner account as you navigate between the Shop, Collection, Decks, Trades, and Arena.
* **Security & Anti-Abuse:** Protect forms, API requests, and account actions against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) and automated abuse.
* **Consent Preferences:** Store your choices made on our cookie banner so we do not ask you on every page load.

## Analytics cookies
With your explicit consent, we use **Google Tag Manager** and **Google Analytics** to collect aggregated statistical data about how visitors and Summoners use our site (such as pages visited, session duration, device type, and general traffic sources).
* **Purpose:** These cookies help Horizon Glitch Studio measure portal performance, identify technical bottlenecks, and improve the overall user experience.
* **Optional:** Analytics cookies are disabled by default until you click **Accept** (or enable them via **Choose**) on our cookie banner.

## Changing your choice
You are in full control of your cookie preferences:
* **Cookie Banner & Settings:** You can accept, reject, or customize non-essential cookies when you first visit the site, and you can update or withdraw your consent at any time via the **Cookie Settings** link in the site footer.
* **Browser Controls:** You can also block or delete cookies directly through your browser settings. Please note that disabling necessary cookies will prevent you from logging in or playing Will of the Void.
