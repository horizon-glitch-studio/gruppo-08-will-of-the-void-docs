# Privacy Policy
**How we handle your personal data.**  
**Last updated:** November 16, 2026

## Who we are
**Will of the Void** is developed and operated by **Horizon Glitch Studio**, based in Turin, Italy ("we", "us", or "our"). We act as the Data Controller for the personal data collected through the official Will of the Void web portal (`willofthevoid.com`) and related game services.

## What data we collect
When you register and play as a Summoner, we collect and process the following categories of personal data:
* **Account & Identity Data:** Username, email address, encrypted password, and date of birth (to verify that you meet the minimum age requirement of 14 years).
* **Gameplay & Preference Data:** Your selected time zone (used to calculate daily bonus resets and cooldowns), match history, Trophies, Orbit tier, card collection, saved decks, Shards balance, and Trade logs.
* **Technical & Usage Data:** IP address, browser type, device information, session identifiers, and interaction events collected via essential cookies, Google Tag Manager, and Google Analytics (subject to your cookie consent).
* **Communication & CRM Data:** Records of your support requests, email verification status, referral invites, and communications managed through our Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system.

## Why we use it
We process your data under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) based on the following legal grounds:
* **Performance of a Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR):** To create and manage your Summoner account, authenticate logins, simulate Arena matches, process card Trades, calculate Season and Orbit rewards, and deliver transactional emails (such as account verification and password resets).
* **Legal Obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR):** To verify age eligibility (14+) and comply with applicable Italian and European laws.
* **Legitimate Interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR):** To prevent cheating, multi-account abuse (such as invite bonus farming), and unauthorized access, as well as to maintain server security and troubleshoot technical issues.
* **Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR):** To analyze portal traffic and user behavior through Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, and to send optional game updates or promotional communications via our CRM.

## How long we keep it
* **Active Account Data:** Retained for as long as your Summoner account remains active.
* **Inactive or Deleted Accounts:** If you delete your account, your personal identifiers (email, date of birth, password) are erased or anonymized within 30 days. Anonymized match logs and historical leaderboard records may be retained to preserve the integrity of past Ranked Seasons and opponent match histories.
* **Analytics & CRM Logs:** Retained in accordance with our Cookie Policy and data retention schedules (up to 14 months for analytics data, unless you withdraw consent earlier).

## Who we share it with
We never sell your personal data. We share data only with trusted third-party service providers acting as Data Processors under strict confidentiality and GDPR-compliant agreements:
* **Cloud Hosting & Infrastructure Providers:** To host the game servers, databases, and web portal within the European Economic Area (EEA).
* **Analytics & Tag Management:** Google Ireland Limited (Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager) to measure portal performance and usage patterns, only if you opt in via our cookie banner.
* **CRM & Email Delivery Services:** To send account verification links, support responses, and—where consented—community announcements.
* **Legal Authorities:** When strictly required by law or to protect our legal rights.

## Your rights
Under the GDPR, you have the right to:
* **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
* **Rectify** inaccurate or incomplete information (note: your time zone can be updated directly in your profile settings once every 30 days).
* **Erase** your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
* **Restrict or Object** to specific processing activities, including withdrawing your consent for analytics or CRM communications at any time.
* **Data Portability**, allowing you to request a structured, machine-readable copy of your account data.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (*Garante per la protezione dei dati personali*) or your local supervisory authority.

## Deleting your account
You can request the permanent deletion of your Summoner account at any time through your account settings or by emailing **support@willofthevoid.com**. Once confirmed, your Ship, card collection, saved decks, Trophies, and unspent Shards will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.
